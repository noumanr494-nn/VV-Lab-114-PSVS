
# PSVS Lab - Requirements

**Project:** Polar Seed Vault System  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** To document the given system requirements for verification.

## Requirements

**S1:** The system shall accept boxes only from registered depositors.

**S2:** Each chamber shall be able to store a large number of boxes.

**S3:** Chambers 1, 2, and 3 shall store a maximum of 50,000 boxes each. Chamber 4 shall store a maximum of 20,000 boxes.

**S4:** Each box shall belong to one depositor. Only that depositor shall be allowed to withdraw the box.

**S5:** In an emergency, the vault authority may release any box to a requesting party.

**S6:** Boxes shall be stored at a safe temperature.

**S7:** The temperature of a chamber shall not rise above -18°C.

**S8:** During maintenance, a chamber may be warmed to 0°C.

**S9:** Samples with low viability shall be flagged for regeneration promptly.

**S10:** A depositor shall not withdraw more than 20% of its boxes of a particular species in one year.

**S11:** A withdrawn box shall be inspected again before it can be deposited back.

**S12:** The system shall maintain a record of all actions.
